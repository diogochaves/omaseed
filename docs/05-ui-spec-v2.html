Design spec v2 · Workstation direction

omaseed, drawn the way flea, omamail and Strata draw

Second draft, built from the reference board. Same scope as v1: main window, details, every sheet and palette, pages, tray, notifications, states, keys and tokens. What changed: a hero row with headline speeds, a borderless rail with tracked labels, taller rows with an outlined cursor, details docked on the right, unboxed key hints, sheets with three equal buttons, centred palettes for pickers, and a translucent ground over the wallpaper. Switch theme and base size above; the wallpaper behind the mocks is a stand-in painted from the theme.

Hero, rail, table, details, footer

The hero carries identity and the two numbers that matter. The rail filters. The table is the work surface. Details dock right at this width and drop below the table under 1100 px. The footer states facts on the left and teaches keys on the right.

9 · v1 → v2

What changed and where it comes from

01
Hero row replaces the chip header.
Mark, name, tracked server line, two headline speeds, search with count, four glyph actions. From omalink, omarqui, omaroll, Strata.
02
Rail without borders, tracked labels, counts in small dim type.
From flea and Strata.
03
Rows 2.4× body, category dimmed after the name, cursor outlined, selection filled.
From flea's table and Strata's outlined cursor. Fewer columns: peers merged, category folded into the name.
04
Details dock on the right, open with a four-value strip.
From Strata's preview pane and flea's key/value block. Below 1100 px they drop under the table.
05
Footer: facts left, unboxed hints right, help pinned.
From flea, omaroll, Strata. No boxed keys anywhere except the tokens page.
06
Sheets with an icon-title-subtitle head and equal buttons.
From Strata's properties sheet. Confirm stays the shell's ConfirmDialog.
07
Palettes for jump, pickers and sort.
From Strata's search. Replaces three menu styles in v1.
08
First run as a page; settings as one modal with a left nav.
From omamail and Strata. Servers live in settings; the server sheet opens from a row.
09
Translucent ground, 1px window edge.
From omaroll and omakade. Requires the Hyprland opt-out rule for default opacity.
10
Primary buttons are accent outlines, not fills.
The kit has no filled button; omakade's Play and Strata's sheet buttons agree.
