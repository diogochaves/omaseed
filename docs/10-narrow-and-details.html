1 · What the app does today

Nothing gives way, so the right-hand edge is cut off

The spec never says how the hero, footer or table behave when narrow, and the window has no minimum width. Every row is laid out at full size and whatever doesn't fit is cut off.

Fig. 1.1: The hero at four widths, alt-speed on, firewalled. Below about 1300 px the mouse can't reach Add, Settings, More or Help. The keys still work.
Fig. 1.2: The whole window at 800 px. The table's right-hand columns are cut off with no way to scroll to them, and Name is the first column to shrink. The footer loses ? help. Details at the bottom take 300 px, leaving four table rows.
Fig. 1.3: At 1500 px, Details is already at the bottom. The spec says it drops below the table "under 1100 px". SplitLayout compares Theme.space(1100) (1283 px at base 14) against the area beside the rail, not the window. So on your desktop Details sits on the right only when the window is wider than about 1515 px.
Hero actions get cut off bugThe RowLayout in Kit/Hero.qml has no minimum widths or priorities, so the last items fall off first, and those are the actions.
Footer loses ? help bugThe hints are one row laid out left to right, so the help key, which sits at the far right, disappears first.
Table cuts columns off bugThe fixed columns keep their width, so Name shrinks to about 120 px, and then ↑ and ETA fall off the right edge.
Breakpoint measured against the wrong width spec gap1283 px beside the rail instead of 1100 px of window at base 12. With the rail hidden (ctrl+b) Details jumps to the right, which is surprising.
Bottom pane has a fixed heightsideHeight: Theme.space(260). You can't make it smaller or larger. That's B1.7.
2 · The hero

Decide what gives way, in what order, and what never does

What never goes: the mark and its status dot, both speed figures, + and ⋯. Everything else has a width below which it gets smaller or moves into ⋯. Its key keeps working, and ⋯ gains a row for it, so the mouse can always reach it.

3 · The footer

? help is pinned right, hints drop from the middle, and facts get shorter

The footer teaches keys, so the key that teaches all the others must never be the one that disappears.

Fig. 3.1: The same footer at 1500, 1150, 900, 700 and 560 px, the hero's steps. Hints drop in reverse order of use (o, d, p, /, space, enter, j k). The facts shorten from "16 torrents · 8 downloading · 1.80 TB free · 2 selected" down to "16 · 2 sel".
4 · The table

Columns step aside in a fixed order; Name keeps its share

Today the fixed columns keep their width, Name shrinks to fit, and whatever is left falls off the right edge. Proposed: Name never drops below 40 % of the table. When the columns don't fit, they hide one at a time, least useful first. It's temporary: the columns you picked with | are kept, and they come back when the window widens. The header of the last visible column shows +2, and clicking it opens the columns palette.

Fig. 4.1: Table area (the width beside the rail) at 1300, 1100, 850, 650 and 480 px. A column goes when keeping it would push Name below 40 % of the row. Order of retreat: ↑, S/P, ETA, Size, ↓. Below 560 px Status becomes its glyph alone, keeping its colour, with the word in a tooltip. Progress is never hidden. It's the column you came for.
5 · Details: right, bottom, and the divider · B1.7

A placement setting, a divider you can drag, and the same things from the keyboard

Omarchy has no convention for dividers inside a window: it tiles windows, not panes. So this is our call. The app is keyboard-first, so anything the mouse can do to the divider, a key can do too.

PlacementSettings → Appearance → Details: auto (the default: right when the window is wide enough, otherwise bottom), right or bottom. I (shift+i) cycles it and the footer confirms it for two seconds. i still shows and hides the pane.
BreakpointFix it to what the spec says: measured against the window, 1100 px at base 12 (1283 at base 14). With the rail visible, that gives the table about 750 px beside a 340 px pane.
DividerStill a 1 px hairline, but it responds across 6 px. On hover it turns accent and the pointer becomes a resize cursor. Dragging shows the size. Double-click resets it. Right pane: 260 to 640 px of width. Bottom pane: 160 px to 60 % of height.
Keyboard< and > make the pane smaller and larger by 40 px, as vim's ctrl+w < > do. Both keys are free today.
MemoryOne size per placement, per server, in state.json next to the table state. Switching placement doesn't lose the other size.
At the bottomA wide, short pane shouldn't stack the right-hand layout into 300 px of height. The header and figures move into a left column and the fields sit in two columns beside them, so Details fits in about 200 px.
Fig. 5.1: Right, at 1300 px, while the divider is being dragged. The 6 px hit area is tinted here so you can see it; in the app only the hairline and the grip change.
Fig. 5.2: Bottom, at 1100 px, after I. The two-column layout, the grip on hover, and the footer note. Six table rows instead of today's four at the same height.
