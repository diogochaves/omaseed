The details pane under the table: one band, full-width tabs

Replaces docs/10 §5 Fig. 5.2 ("two-column layout") for the bottom dock only. The right dock is unchanged.

What was wrong

Docked at the bottom, the head (name, category · tags · added) and the four-up strip sat in a 280-unit left column with the tabs beside it. The column's content is about two short blocks tall, so most of it was empty. Meanwhile the tabs beside it lost those 280 units: Files cut long names, and General dropped to one column sooner.

The ruling

Under the table, the head and the strip share one band across the top of the pane, in the right dock's order (head, then strip) but side by side. The tabs and their body take the whole width below it.

  • General's two-column switch stays at 620 units of body width. The body is now the full pane, so it switches to two columns at any usual window width.
