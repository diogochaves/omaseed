First run, designed as one flow

The setup screens grew one step at a time: a page shape from spec v2 Fig. 15, fields added in T4.6, a cookie option and headings added today. This board treats them as one piece: what a person sees from the first launch until their torrents show up. Diogo approved every recommended option (A1, B1, C1, D1, E1) and it is built. One detail differs from the mocks: "skip test" became "save anyway", shown only after a failed test, since the board never said where the esc menu would be.

Today

  1. The top bar belongs to the library, not to setup. It says NO SERVER, shows empty speeds, a filter with a count of 0, alt-speed, add, settings and help. None of them work before a server exists. Spec Fig. 15 draws the page with no bar at all.
  2. Everything hangs from the top edge. Step 3 is three lines with about 900 px of nothing under them. A short page centred in the window reads as intended; one pinned to the top reads as unfinished.
  3. No welcome. The first thing a new user sees is a form-like question. The app never says what it is, and the server search runs invisibly.
  4. Progress is a footer caption. "step 2 of 3" sits bottom-left in small dim text. The steps have no names, so you can't tell what is left.
  5. No ending. After step 3 the page simply disappears. There is no moment that says "connected to strict, 3 torrents".

The proposal: a setup surface

One surface for every moment when there is no library to show yet. The window loses the library's chrome; a single column, 36 base units wide, sits in the middle of the window, with its text left-aligned. At the top of the column: the mark and the name, then the named steps, then the step's question. Keys stay where they are today (j k, enter, tab, esc) and show in the footer.

The choices inside it

A · The top bar during setup

B · A welcome on first launch

  • recommended B1 · Welcome screen, once. Screen 0 above. Shows the name, the one-line pitch, and the server search as it runs. Never shown again after setup.
  • B2 · No separate screen. The mark, name and pitch sit above step 1's question, and the search result shows in the rows.
  • B3 · A splash on every launch while the first sync loads. Not proposed: after setup, the library is what people open the app for, and a splash only puts time in front of it.

C · Where the column sits

  • recommended C1 · Centred both ways, text left-aligned. Each step is a different height, so the brand row moves a little between steps. Short steps like 3 look intended.
  • C2 · Centred across, fixed at one-third down. No shift between steps; short steps still sit a little high.

D · Progress

  • recommended D1 · Named steps at the top of the column ("✓ server · 2 sign in · 3 links"), with "step 2 of 3" kept in the footer.
  • D2 · The footer caption only, as today.

E · An ending

  • recommended E1 · A "done" screen (screen 4) that names the server, counts the torrents and teaches three keys.
  • E2 · Straight into the library after step 3, as today.

Where else the surface fits

The same column, mark and centring work for any window with no library in it. These are not part of this proposal's build; they show why the surface is worth getting right once.
