What gives when the window is narrow

Three places where the 2026-09-24 review found the window running out of room at its 560 px floor: the hero with filter chips up, the stats strip in the details band, and the settings rows. Each section shows today first, then the options side by side at the same widths.

The mocks are measured, not guessed. A red frame and a cut off flag mean the row really is wider than the window in this page's layout. The mocks scale with the base toggle, so try base 12 and 14.

Q1 · Hero at its narrowest

With the rail hidden, active filters become chips in the hero, and at the floor they don't fit

Below 600 px the hero is on its last step: the mark, the dot, both speeds, the / button, the alt-speed glyph, + and ⋯. The rail is hidden too, so each active filter (status, category, tag, tracker) shows as a chip folded to its glyph and ✕.

Measured on the real Hero.qml, with speeds reading 4.21 MB/s and 812 KB/s: the row needs 562 px with no chips and about 62 px more per chip. At 600 px no chip fits. At the 560 px floor the row is 2 px too wide before any chip. So every option below also shortens the speeds on the last step (↓4.21M ↑812K), the short form the alt-speed chip already uses. That change alone makes the empty row fit again.

Today. No filters and one filter at 600 px; none and three at 560 px. Alt-speed on.
Q2 · Details stats strip

Under the table, the strip gets half the band and all four facts elide

Below 1100 px the details pane docks under the table, and its header becomes one band: the name on the left, the four facts on the right. The band gives the strip at most half the pane, so at 560 px it gets 280 px for size, done, ratio and ETA. Every value elides, which is worse than showing fewer of them. A dashed red outline marks each value that is cut.

Today. The band at 700 and 560 px, a torrent 48.9 % done.
Q3 · Settings rows

The section list keeps its 175 px, so each row's text gets what's left after the control

At a 560 px window the settings sheet shows the section list and the rows side by side. A row then has about 300 px for its glyph, text and control. A 128 px field or a three-way switch leaves the description too narrow, and it breaks one word at a time.
