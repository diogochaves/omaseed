A selection bar, and a details dock that folds

Two asks from the first manual pass. M9: a row of action buttons that shows while torrents are selected, the way qui and qBittorrent's toolbar offer them, so the mouse has what enter gives the keyboard. M10: the divider above the details gets a two-stage double-click. It folds the dock away to give the table the whole window, then unfolds it to the size you had.

§1 is the real build, shot in an omabox with the app's demo data. Everything after it is a mock in the v2 kit. qui was read for behaviour only; the bar here is drawn from omaseed's own kit and glyphs.

The actions sit behind enter, and i leaves nothing behind

A plain click selects one row. space, ctrl+click and shift+click add to the selection, and j k move only the cursor, the outlined row. Every action applies to the selection, or to the cursor row when nothing is selected. The mouse reaches the actions only through a right-click. The footer counts the selection and teaches six keys.

Two selected, the cursor on a third. Nothing on screen offers an action to the mouse. The footer says "2 selected" and lists the keys.

The recommendation first, then each choice with its options. The glyphs are the kit's where it has one; the ones marked new are drawn to the kit's rules (24-unit grid, square ends, mitred joins) and would join Kit/Icon.qml.

Recommended. 1440 × 780. Two torrents selected, the details at the bottom. The bar floats over the table, centred on it, 12 u above the divider. Hovering recheck shows its key. With seven rows the bar sits over empty table; with a long list, the table grows room under its last row (M9.6).
Close up, at 100 %. The count, then start and stop, the checks, the four "where and how" actions, open folder, ⋯ for the rest of the menu, and delete in red at the end. Hover and the tooltip use the kit's fills: 8 % hover, a hairline border.

A · one or more selected recThe bar appears with the first selected row and goes when the selection clears (esc, or a click on empty table). A plain click selects, so a mouse user sees it on the first click. Moving the cursor with j k never shows it: the cursor is not a selection.
B · also for the cursor rowThe bar shows whenever the table has rows. It becomes a permanent toolbar that always covers part of the table.
C · two or moreOne selected torrent keeps today's screen; the bar is only for batches. A mouse user then has no buttons for a single torrent.

3 · M10 · The details dock

Double-click folds and unfolds; dragging finds the default

Diogo decided the rule. Double-click never goes to the default size any more. It folds the dock, and unfolds it to whatever size you had. The default is reached by dragging, with a snap when you pass near it.
