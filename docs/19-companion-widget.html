omaseed in the bar

Round 4 answers your drag-and-drop and paste questions with tests in a box, drops the text field from Add, and moves "open omaseed" into the head. Round 3 took your notes on round 2. In the bar, the mark and the speed are two switches you can have together; the speed shows down, up or both; the mark lights in one colour or in the app's blue and green. The popup is one view with sections that fold, adding torrents inline, the week strip in, and more of the "keeping alive" stats. Alt-speed turns the speeds and the mark's square the app's alt-speed colour.

Round 4: what the box said, and what changed

Can the icon take a dropped .torrent?No, tested. A throwaway plugin with a drop area on its bar icon and another inside its popup, in a box (Omarchy 4.0.4, Quickshell 0.3.1, Hyprland 0.56.2), and a .torrent dragged from Nautilus. On the bar icon, the drop area never heard the drag (not even "entered"). Into the open popup, no drag can start: the press on Nautilus lands on the popup's outside-click layer. Your omarqui fork hit a third problem (a drop area on the bar chip made the popup close on its own); that one did not happen here. Dropping works only on a plugin's own small surface (as sridhar.shelf does), which a bar widget is not. So dropping is out; the app is already where a double-clicked or browser-opened .torrent lands

Do we need a text field?No. Add lists what is ready (pasted, the clipboard on open, today's files in ~/Downloads), with Browse… for the rest

2 · The popup: one view, sections that fold

The head and the "now" block never fold; every other section has a header you click (or space on it) to fold it. A folded section keeps one line: its most useful fact. The fold state is remembered, so the popup stays the size you made it. Click the section headers below.
