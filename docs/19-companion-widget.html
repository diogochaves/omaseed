Declared in the manifest's barWidget.schema, rendered by the shell's own form (Q11). The widget reads $XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/omaseed/status.json with a watched FileView (no polling, no subprocess at rest; schema in docs/07 Fig. 36, unchanged, with no compatibility across versions: Q12) and acts through io.github.diogochaves.omaseed.Status over D-Bus. It touches widget.alive every 30 s so the app hides its tray icon under trayIconMode auto (Q7). Plugin id chaves.omaseed (Q8). It never talks to qBittorrent and holds no credential.
