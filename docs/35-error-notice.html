The error notice

Board 33 was ruled "all approved as recommended" except one thing: the error notice's look. Its content rules stand (D21: a plain headline naming the server or torrent, one line on what happens next, at most two actions, the technical text behind "details" with copy) and so do its words (D23). This board changes what the notice looks like and where it sits, so it reads as a part of the window instead of text that fell into it.

§1 has real screenshots. §2 onwards are mocks at base 14 in Theme roles, at 1180 px wide (narrow ones at 720 and 480, the app's minimum); use the theme switch for them. The server is "private" at 127.0.0.1:8091, as in board 33.

The whole window today (shot 01)

What the window does everywhere else, and the notice doesn't

Every region in v2 (Fig. 1, Fig. 23) runs edge to edge and meets its neighbours at a 12 % hairline: the hero has one under it, the rail one on its right, the table head one under it, the details pane one on its left, the footer one above it. Fills mark a state: 4 % for a band that's set apart (the rail's servers band, board 33 D16; board 28's error block), 8 % hover, 18 % selected. Popups float: opaque, with an accent edge. The notice is the only thing that does neither.

  1. No box. No fill and no edge: its headline, line and details read as one more column of text, and the table header under it reads as part of it.
  2. It sits in a margin nothing else uses. 16 px in from the sides, 10 px gaps above and below: the only inset thing in a window of full-width strips.
  3. It moves the rail and the table with nothing to show why. When it comes, when details open, when it goes. With no edge, the move looks like the layout slipping.
2 · four places for it

Where a server's notice could live, each drawn in the window

Each option below uses the same box: the notice's own 4 % fill, a 2 px rule in its kind's colour on the left edge, a hairline where it meets the content. They differ in where the box goes and what moves. Each shows the notice folded while omaseed retries (yellow), unfolded after a refused sign-in (red), and at 720 px.

Severity, as drawn here (N3): yellow "!" while omaseed retries by itself (nothing lost, nothing to do), red "✕" when it has stopped and needs you. Board 33's N1 drew "didn't answer" red; its A3 drew the same state yellow in the add sheet. This board makes them agree.

A · A band across the window, under the hero

recommended
For. It is one more full-width strip, framed the way v2 frames every region: the hero's hairline above, its own below. A server problem is about the whole window, and the band says so. The rail and the table move together, by the same amount, so nothing goes out of line.
Against. The body moves down by the band's height (≈ 60 px at base 14) when the server drops, and up when it comes back. Unfolding details moves it again, on your click.

B · Boxed inside the content column, beside the rail

not recommended
For. The rail doesn't move. The notice sits over what it talks about: "the list below is from 23:26".
Against. The rail's STATUS head and the table head no longer line up: the misalignment that made board 33's mock look broken, now on purpose. A server problem is the window's, not the table's. Beside the details pane it gets narrower still (see A's three-column mock for the width it would lose). At 720 the actions drop under the line: four rows.

C · Docked to the footer, opening upward over the content

not recommended
For. Nothing ever moves: the footer takes the notice's fill and rule; details open over the table's last rows and close with esc. It is where D22 already puts an action's failure.
Against. The least-read line of the window carries its most important state, at caption size. Folded, the line under the headline doesn't fit: D21's "one line on what happens next" becomes a fragment. The footer's facts and key hints are gone while the server is down.

D · The hero takes the error state

not recommended
For. Nothing is pushed down: while the server is down, ↓ and ↑ read "—" anyway, so the notice takes their place, and the hero takes the notice's fill and rule. Details open as a popup under the hero.
Against. At 1180 the line already elides; at 720 the search folds and the line goes, and at 480 the headline is all that's left. The hero changes colour with the server, which is a lot of signal for "retrying in 7 s". Sheets and the footer can't use the same shape, so the family splits.

3 · recommended

A in full: the band, its states and widths

place
Under the hero, the full width of the window, over every column (rail, table, details pane). The body's top is the band's bottom: no margins.
box
Theme.fill(0.04); a Theme.space(2) rule in the kind's colour on the left edge, full height; a hairline (0.12) under it. The hero's hairline is the top edge.
padding
space(9) top, space(10) bottom, space(16) right and left of the rule (the hero's 16): ≈ 60 px folded at base 14.
row 1
The kind's glyph (font.body, kind colour), the headline (font.body, 600, brightForeground), the actions at the right (font.small, accent; "details ▸" in dimForeground, last).
row 2
The line (font.small, foreground), aligned with the headline's first letter, not the glyph. It wraps; it never elides.
details
Under the line, a block at a further 0.04 (so 0.08 over the window), font.caption, dimForeground; copy at its bottom right.
yellow !
omaseed is retrying on its own: didn't answer, couldn't be found, taking too long. The hero's dot, the rail's server count and the band agree.
red ✕
It has stopped and needs you: sign-in refused, refused omaseed (403), certificate (Q27), can't parse. And any action that failed.
accent ·
Information that needs no action; Kit/Banner stays for the one-liners (alternative limits, a burst).
one at a time
A later server problem replaces the words in place; the band doesn't go and come back.
keys
R retries (as today), esc folds details when the band has the focus; tab reaches its actions after the hero's.
4 · one family

The same box in a sheet and from the footer

D22's other places, in A's box. The rule for all of them: the notice is a band the full width of what holds it, between that thing's head and its body, with a line on the side that meets the content. In a sheet that's under the sheet's head; from the footer, it docks above the footer and the line goes on top.

5 · against board 33

What changes, if the recommendations stand

Changed

  • The look: a band with its own fill, a kind rule and a hairline, full width, no inset (N1, N2). Board 33's .ntc mocks (N1–N5) are drawn again here.
  • Severity follows "retrying" (yellow) or "needs you" (red) for server problems; board 33's N1 had "didn't answer" red (N3).
  • In a sheet: under the head, as A3 and N3 drew it; D22's text said "above its buttons" and N5 drew it under the url field (N7).
  • From the footer: the same band docked above it, not a panel inset 16 px (N8).

Unchanged

  • D21's content: headline naming the server or torrent, one line, at most two actions, details with copy.
  • D22's scopes: a server's problem in the window, an action in a sheet in that sheet, an action from the table in the footer first, a torrent's own error in its details (board 28), a desktop notice when you're away.
  • D23's words, all of them; the one retry figure.

What it takes, when built

  • Kit/Notice.qml (board 33's, plus): edge ("bottom" | "top", where the hairline goes), stacked from its own width, the countdown in a fixed-width slot, the 120 ms height animation (N6) off under reduced motion.
  • MainView: the body anchored to the notice's bottom with no margins; DisconnectedBanner becomes the Notice.
  • AddSheet, ServerSheet: the Notice under the head, its side margins the sheet's padding negated.
  • tst_Notice.qml: stacks below its width, keeps its height while the countdown counts and when the kind changes.
