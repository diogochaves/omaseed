omaseed · board 38 · Q19 amended · #89

Text colours read at 4.5:1 on the window, in every theme

Every colour omaseed paints text and glyphs with is lifted towards the theme's foreground until it reads at 4.5:1 (WCAG 2.2 AA, normal-size text) against the window background: dimForeground (it was 3:1) and now accent, red, yellow, orange, green, cyan, blue and magenta; the state* roles read those. A colour that already reads is kept byte for byte. Across the 27 installed themes, 64 of 243 role colours move, in 25 themes; 2 keep every colour.

Generated by scripts/dev/contrast-board.py from each build's omaseed --theme-roles, offscreen, one throwaway theme state per theme. Before: main at 3b730c2 (3:1 on dimForeground only). After: 340091c (4.5:1 on the nine text roles).

§1

The rule

  • One floor, Theme::kTextContrastFloor = 4.5, cited from docs/DECISIONS.md Q19.
  • Same mechanism as before (Theme::liftToContrast): the colour is mixed towards foreground in 1/256 steps and the first step that reads is kept, so the hue moves as little as it can.
  • Measured against the opaque window background, as Q19 always was: the ground moves with the wallpaper behind the window's alpha.
  • Not against the row fills (§3). Faint text (40 %) stays for disabled things, which WCAG exempts; the "via" before a path in the command palette is dimForeground now.
  • State roles follow the role they read: checked, every theme, both builds.

§3

Why the window and not the row fills

Captions are told from body text by their distance from foreground. With the floor on the window, the median contrast between dimForeground and foreground is 2.43:1 (it was 2.94:1). Lifting against the selected row's fill as well (the foreground at the shell's fillSelected, 18 % on a stock Omarchy) would leave it at 1.66:1: captions would read as body text. Coloured text on selected and pressed rows gets its own fix (#90).
