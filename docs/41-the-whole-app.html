The whole app全体

omaseed is something you glance at, not a spreadsheet you operate. It opens on the questions people bring to a torrent client (does anything need me, what is arriving and when, what landed, am I giving back, what is resting) and answers each one in words before numbers. The window has one frame that never changes: where you are down the left, what you are looking at along the top in one sentence, what is live along the bottom. Columns, raw names, peers and keys are one step away, never on screen by default. The same design wears two skins: Omarchy's, quiet and monospace, following any theme; and the console's, BASELINE's plates, cuts, labels and lamps, for a desk that runs omarchy-console.

a proposal: nothing here is ruled built from boards 39, 40 and today's main two skins, one design colours: omaseed --theme-roles, 4.5:1 example data

§1Why 39 and 40 felt off

My read of your "it still seems a bit off", not your words. Tell me where I'm wrong in the notes at the end.

board 39
Good content, crowded frame

Rows that speak, the story in details and the add sheet's checks were the strong part. But six layouts, each with its own chrome, were stacked onto a top bar that was already full, and the servers moved from layout to layout.

board 40
Placement without a point of view

Seven frames moved the same ten controls around. None of them said what the app is for, so each looked like today's window rearranged. A frame should follow from a thesis, not be the thesis.

board 41
One idea, finished everywhere

This board picks one direction, carries it through every surface (frame, library, add, settings, menu, actions, details), and draws it twice so the look and the design can be judged separately. You take what you want from here.

§2Five laws

Short enough to check a screen against, as BASELINE's rules are.

law 1
Questions, not columns

The rail's first group is the five questions. "Library" shows every torrent grouped under them. A status word like "stalledUP" never reaches the screen.

law 2
One frame, always

Rail left, title top, status line bottom, in every layout and width. The servers live at the rail's foot and nowhere else. A layout changes only the content.

law 3
Words first

Each row says the one thing that matters on the right ("7 min", "2.4×", "files missing") and one sentence under the name. The numbers are in details.

law 4
Keys on demand

Menus, tooltips, the palette and ? teach the keys. Nothing else paints them. Holding alt shows every key on the controls for as long as it's held.

law 5
Calm until it needs you

Colour marks what needs you and what is moving. Motion only for work in progress. A window with nothing wrong reads as still.

Omarchy skin bends
Nothing of v2's rules

It changes v2's structure (§7) but keeps every theme rule in docs/ARCHITECTURE.md: Theme roles only, the four fills, hairlines at 12 %, base-size, the Omarchy font.

Console skin bends
Six v2 rules
  • Solid fills above 22 % (the accent's buttons, the lamp, selected chips).
  • Cut corners, against "square".
  • Three faces that aren't the Omarchy font (OFL and Apache 2.0, from Google Fonts, as the console installs them).
  • Upper-case tracked labels and kana.
  • The state colours give way to the console's role budget.
  • Motion beyond fades (decode, flicker, the block cycle).

§7What this would replace

Listed, not decided. Each needs your ruling, and each "yes" becomes its own build board as before.

v2's hero

The hero (mark, server line, speeds, tabs, filter and five buttons) becomes the title bar: a title, its sentence, filter, display and add. v2 stays frozen; a ruling here supersedes it in DECISIONS.

The rail's status group

The status filters (all, downloading, seeding, completed, stopped, active, stalled, checking, errored) become the five questions. Every status stays reachable by the filter (/is:stalled) and the palette.

The view tabs (#24)

library, seed and log move to the rail's first rows; ` and ~ stay.

C12's hero ▾ and the footer's words

The servers stay where C12 put them (the rail's foot) and keep ctrl+s; the hero's "NAME ▾" goes with the hero, and the footer's key hints go (law 4). C12's ErrorWords and the notice's words stay.

docs/35 N1

The notice spans the content under the title, not the window; the rail beside it stays usable (as board 40's F10 a).

The selection bar

While torrents are selected, the status line becomes the action bar (§4 actions). The floating bar goes.

The details' tabs

General, Files, Trackers, Peers, Web seeds become Story, Files, Swarm, Trackers, Facts. Today's General rows live in Facts; web seeds join Trackers.

Board 39's D-calls and board 40's F-calls
