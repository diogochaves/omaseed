After a move: where it says so

Diogo walked boards 42 and 43 by hand in a box on 21 October. The moves worked; five things around them did not read well. A one-torrent move says it landed at the bottom left of the window, far from the row it moved. A move that fails says so in the same place. The places list has no way in for the mouse. Selecting several rows with the mouse works but nothing says how. And closing a move's summary takes "move back" away for good, although omaseed knew where everything was a moment before. This board proposes a fix for each, in four parts.

§1 is today, in shots from a box running main (move-library.sh's library). §2–§5 are the proposals, mocks at base 14 in Theme roles (use the theme switch). The torrents are the test library's own. Diogo's item numbers from his pass are kept: 1 and 7 (§2), 2 (§3), 3 (§4), 5 (§5). The calls are D1–D9 in §6; at the ruling they become one C entry in DECISIONS.

What the pass saw

Item 3 (selecting several rows) has no shot: the pass used the filter and space, which worked. The mouse already does it, as the table's code says: ctrl+click adds or removes a row, shift+click selects a range, a middle click adds or removes. Nothing on screen says so.

2 · items 1 and 7 · where an action's outcome shows

Say it where the torrent is, or where the selection bar is

An action on a torrent ends in one of three ways: it lands at once, it fails, or it becomes a job long enough to need the band. The third keeps the band under the top bar (board 42): a job is about many torrents and lasts minutes. The first two are about the rows just acted on, and today they end up in the footer, the one part of the window the eye isn't on. Four places were weighed:

3 · item 2 · the places list

A visible way in, for the mouse too

The places list is the sheet's most useful part and the least visible. It keeps opening on ↓ and on typing (C16 L4); what's missing is a control you can see and click.

Other ways weighed: opening on any click in the field (fast, but a click to fix one character opens a list over the checks), and opening on focus (the list would cover the checks every time the sheet opens). Both are offered in D5.

4 · item 3 · several rows with the mouse

It works; make it findable

Three things could tell a mouse user how to pick several rows. Each costs something different.

Other ways weighed: a full tick column always shown (clear, but 24 px of every row for a thing used now and then), and the footer's key hints switching to mouse words when the pointer was used last (no new UI, but the footer is the place this board moves things away from). Both are in D6.

5 · item 5 · move back after close

Keep the door where the record is

A job's record holds each torrent's old save path and survives a restart (C15 M13), but only until its summary is closed. Closing the band should close the band, not the way back. That means keeping a short history of finished moves per server, after the close: for each, its title, when, and each torrent's old and new path, a few KB. Then three doors, each where someone would look for it:

  • How long. The history keeps the last 20 moves or 30 days, whichever ends first (D8). A move past that is gone, and so is its move back.
  • Moved since. A torrent moved again since, by omaseed or anything else, is left out of the move back and listed as such, as E2 already says for the summary. When none is left, the door says so instead of opening an empty sheet.
  • The summary's close gets a one-time line in the strip (§2): "move back stays in the torrents' details and the log".
