The whole app, on v0.3

Board 41's thesis holds: omaseed is something you glance at, not a spreadsheet you operate. It opens on the questions people bring to a torrent client (does anything need me, what is arriving and when, what landed, am I giving back, what is resting) and answers each in words before numbers, in one frame that never changes: where you are down the left, what you are looking at along the top in one sentence, what is live along the bottom. 41 was drawn on 17 October. Since then v0.3 shipped folder moves and the Move sheet, the after-a-move record, servers in any state with the error notice, settings scope, one menu for the tray and the widget, and board 44's disk work is being built. This board keeps 41's thesis and five laws and redraws them over all of that, in the two skins you ruled: Omarchy, the default, and the console, your BASELINE.

§1 is today, in shots from a box running main. §3 is the window, live, in both skins: every surface v0.3 has, in the new frame. The calls are H1–H18 in §11; they replace 41's G1–G12, and each says which ruling it would supersede. Nothing is built before you rule.

2 · 41's thesis, kept

Five laws, one change

law 1
Questions, not columns

The rail's first group is the five questions; the library is grouped under them. A word like "stalledUP" never reaches the screen.

law 2 · tightened
One frame, always

Rail left, title top, status line bottom, in every display and width. Bands (a server's notice, a job) sit under the title across the content; the rail beside them stays usable. The servers live at the rail's foot and in the status line; a sheet that acts on one server names it (C12's chip).

law 3
Words first

Each row says the one thing that matters on the right ("7 min", "2.4×", "didn't move") and one sentence under the name. Numbers are in details and the table.

law 4
Keys on demand

Menus, tooltips, the palette and ? teach the keys. Nothing else paints them. Holding alt shows every key on the controls.

law 5
Calm until it needs you

Colour marks what needs you and what is moving. Motion only for work in progress. A window with nothing wrong reads as still.

Law 2 changed because v0.3 has more than one thing that must speak across the window: the server's notice (C12), a job's band (C15), the outcome strip (C18). 41 had only the notice.

5 · the window's ground on Omarchy 4.5 (#113)

Opaque, like every other app now

Omarchy 4.5 makes apps opaque by default: the default-opacity tag is gone. omaseed draws its own ground at the shell launcher's alpha (Q1: [launcher] background-alpha, 95 % on a stock Omarchy), so among opaque apps it is the one that shows the wallpaper. Popups, sheets and menus are opaque already (Q1, ARCHITECTURE rule 4).

super+backspace on 4.5 runs omarchy-hyprland-window-transparency-toggle: it tags the window transparent, and Hyprland draws the whole window at 0.985 when focused and 0.96 when not. It knows nothing of omaseed's own alpha, so on today's ground the two multiply (0.95 × 0.96 ≈ 0.91 unfocused). On an opaque ground the toggle does what it does for every app.

Try ground in §3's strip, in both skins: the console's plates and cuts need the ground behind them to be still, more than the Omarchy skin does.

a · recommended
Opaque by default

Like 4.5's apps. super+backspace still makes it see-through, through Hyprland, as for any app. Appearance keeps a "translucent" choice that reads the launcher's alpha, as Q1 does today.

b
Keep Q1

The launcher's alpha. omaseed keeps matching the shell's own surfaces, and stays the one see-through app.

c
Follow the desktop's version

Opaque on 4.5 and later, the launcher's alpha before. Right on both, but a rule nobody can see.

6 · search (#145) and create (#146): where they live

A place for search, a verb for create

This board only places them. Their sheets are boards 48 and 49.

#145 · H16 ·
Search is a view in the rail

After library, seed and log: search (ctrl+f), a place you go to and come back from, since results keep arriving as each plugin answers. Its content is the rows display, so a result reads like a torrent: the name, one sentence, seeds as the fact on the right, "in your library" or "on disk" marked. add… opens today's add sheet. It searches the server omaseed is on. Plugins are installed and turned on in Settings › qBittorrent › Search plugins. : "search…" goes there too, and the add sheet's empty state offers it.

Weighed: a tab in the add sheet (search is browsing, not adding; a sheet closes), and the palette only (no place for results to wait in).

#146 · H17 ·
Create is an add verb, and a job

+ add gets a ▾ with "add a torrent or magnet…" and "create a torrent from a folder…" (ctrl+n); : "create torrent…" and a rail folder row's menu ("create a torrent from this folder…") lead to the same sheet. Hashing a big folder is a job, so it shows in the band a move uses, with its % and cancel . A finished public torrent seeds where its files are; a private one is saved for the upload (#146's round trip).

Weighed: a rail view (create is something you do now and then, not a place), the palette only (nobody would find it).
