1 · D1 D14 · the mark and its menu

Settings in sight, and a menu that grows out of the mark

D1 Settings is hard to find

The "omaseed ▾" mark doesn't read as a menu with settings in it; today Settings is reached by ,, the mark's menu and the tray.

Recommended a. The rail's foot is where a settings gear is looked for, it stays in sight folded (a gear alone), and it shares its row with D9's fold toggle, so the status line stays a status line (D17.3). Adds a foot to the rail (C24 H2).

D14 The mark's menu as an extension of the mark

The mark stays lit and the menu grows down from it as one shape; its own "omaseed" head goes (the mark says it), and the version line, 0.5.0 · public · qBittorrent 5.3.0, leads, then servers and the rest. All three below do that; they differ in the shape.

Why b. At the rail's 214 px (a) the rows' keys crowd, and from a folded rail (48 px) the menu can't be "as wide as the rail" at all. One outline round mark and menu reads as one shape at any rail width; the lit mark carries the selected fill (C30's selected ground, measured), the menu the window's opaque ground. c loses the rail behind it, so a server switch from the menu can't be seen happening in the rail.

Recommended b. One accent outline, mark and menu, 292 px, the same from a folded rail. Changes C24 H9's head for this menu ("omaseed" over its version line, board 46's .mh).

2 · D3 D9 D4 D5 D16 · the rail

A rail you can size, fold, right-click, tint, and that follows the view

D3 A resizable rail

Drag its edge, as with the details pane.

One way to draw it. The edge lights in the accent under the pointer (the details pane's split does the same), the width shows while dragging. 160 px keeps a name of a dozen letters and its count; 360 is the most that leaves the list its room at 1180. Under 120 the drag folds it (the 48 px rail), so folding and sizing are one gesture. The width is remembered with the window's state, per window, not per server.

Recommended a (one option drawn, b is "no"). New; supersedes nothing.

D9 It folds by itself, and a visible toggle

With D3's resizable rail: it folds by itself under some width; and a visible fold toggle (today only ctrl+b).

The rule, the same in every option: the rail folds by itself when the list would get narrower than 520 px (the details pane counted, while open), and unfolds when there is room again, unless you folded it by hand; a hand fold or unfold holds until the next hand one. That is F13's narrow window: the content keeps its room before anything is crushed.

Recommended a. The toggle sits with settings at the rail's foot (D1 a), where the rail's own controls belong; b puts a rail control in the content's head, c can't be found until hovered. Amends #167 assumed 4 (ctrl+b the only way to fold).

D4 A server's menu in the rail

Right-click on a server in the rail opens a server menu. Which rows?

omaseed is signed in to one server at a time, so rows that act on a server's qBittorrent (its alternative speeds, stop all, start all) are on the current one's menu only; another server's menu leads with "switch to it". Remove… asks first, as Settings › Servers does today.

Recommended a. Everything you'd do to a server from where it is named, without opening Settings. Adds to C12 and C24 H15 (the servers at the rail's foot); none superseded.

D5 The rail follows the view

Library and seed keep now, categories, folders, tags, trackers. Log: its levels in their place, the filter where the torrent filter sits (the same visual language), and "+ add ▾" becomes "export log". Search: to decide after section 5. qBittorrent's log has four levels (normal, info, warning, critical); the rail holds all four, ticked as the log's chips are today (1–4).

Search, only a sketch. The rail would hold what narrows the answers: each plugin with how it answered (count, slow, didn't), the kinds qBittorrent's search plugins sort into, and recent searches. Section 5 of the pass (search) comes first; this is marked as a first idea and its call (D5s) is "decide then".

Recommended D5 a for the log; D5s a, search after section 5. Export log… saves the lines as shown (the filter and levels applied) to a text file. Supersedes docs/32 L1 (the log takes the body's whole width) and its bar of level chips; the lines, keys and following stay.

D16 The surface tint, back

It left Settings › Appearance with the hero (section 1's box 6 rejected that). How a tint fits the new frame, where it applies, and that it still passes C30's contrast. Before, docs/08 §5 tinted the rail and the details at 4 % or 8 %.

Contrast. A rail on 4 % is a 4 % block: its rows' text and fills read on C30's nested grounds (blockSelected, blockSelectedCursor, blockPressed, #188); on 8 % they are the hover set (hoverIdle … hoverPressed). Both sets are already measured to 4.5:1 in all 28 themes, so a: rail-only needs no new ground. b puts the title bar's tools and the status line's chips on a tint too, and the bands under the title (a notice's own 8 % of its colour) sit against a tinted head, which muddies them. c is today's measure plus the details' grounds.

Recommended a. The rail is the one lasting surface beside the list, and its grounds are already measured. Supersedes #167 assumed 6 (the tint went with the hero); docs/08 §5's two surfaces become one.

3 · D2 D6 D8 D18 · title bar and status line

D6 Alternative speeds on: a clearer sign

Recommended a. The sign sits with the speeds (D2 decides where) and says why they are slow: each speed against its cap. The tint is the state's 18 %, so the word reads on it by C30's tint rule. Changes docs/31 D5's chip (the word and limits, "a little fainter").

D8 Burst and turtle, both at hand

qBittorrent keeps one set of alternative limits, so today it is either burst or turtle (C4 names the set from its numbers). Two presets, either one on at any time, omaseed writing the one you pick as the alternative limits?

The schedule is qBittorrent's: at 23:00 it turns on whatever set is stored. So what is stored between bursts decides what the night gets. In a, turtle is written back when a burst ends, so the night is always the turtle; in b, the last pick stays, so a burst at 15:00 makes the night a burst. While omaseed is closed nothing writes: a burst running when it quits is ended first (turtle written back), and one found running at the next start is ended at its time. Other clients still see one set; C4's naming stays for a set someone else wrote.

Recommended a. Burst is a moment, turtle a habit with a schedule; keeping turtle at rest keeps the schedule meaning what it says. Amends C4 D1–D2 (one set, named from its numbers) for omaseed's own presets, and C13's alternative-limits row, which becomes the choice.

D18 The filter field, open and typed into

It reads as a cramped box; draw it properly. F21 makes it grow; this is its look.

Recommended a. One field across the free room says "you are typing a filter" at once; a second line (b) moves the list each time it opens. Changes board 46's .tool.find (176 px, its words cut); F21 is the fix that makes room.

4 · D17 · acting on a selection

A status bar that stays one, and a real action bar

Your words: the bottom bar is a STATUS bar, that may hold some actions, like the turtle icon. When selecting torrents we should have a REAL action bar, with the most important actions, with icons and so on, like we had before but better. Base it on the new right-click menu. "Before" is board 17's floating bar (icons only, over the list); the new menu is board 46's menuHtml() as F15 builds it: icons on every row, transfer ›, queue ›, copy › as submenus.

D17.1 Where the action bar is

What a's bar holds, and why. The menu's first rows, in its own groups: stop or start (p; both while the selection holds running and stopped, as H8 has it), recheck · move…, category ›, tags › · limits…, more › · delete… in red · esc. Category and tags open the same submenus the menu has, upward; more opens the menu's other rows (reannounce, open folder, transfer ›, queue ›, copy ›), so nothing in the menu is missing from the bar. The count and ledger lead (#24 T1; a click on the ledger to the seed console, as H8). The outcome strip floats just above the bar (C18 D1, C30's show); with no selection it takes the bar's place, as before H8. The bar never covers the last row: the list keeps room under it, as the table does for the strip (C30).

Recommended a. Board 17's place, with words, the menu's groups and its submenus: "like before but better", and the status line is left alone. Supersedes C24 H8 (the status line becomes the action bar); docs/17's floating bar comes back, rebuilt on F15's menu.

D17.3 The status line while a selection is on

8 · D19 · moves

Move back takes back what the move carried

D19 Move back and loose files

A folder move carries the folder's loose files (M17), but move back (L11) returns only the torrents and says The 2 loose files carried stay at …; getting them back means a new folder move by hand. The move keeps the list it carried (#98): move back carries back exactly those files, under the same tick (on by default), never others found there since.

The one real choice is a file on the list that changed at the target after the move (someone edited readme.txt there). M17 leaves a file changed since the dry run where it is; a follows that rule, so a changed file stays and is named; b carries it anyway, since it was the move's. Either way a file put there since and never carried stays, and "move back this one only" (one torrent, from details) carries no loose files.

Recommended a. The same carry, run the other way, with M17's own safety. Amends C16 L11 (move back returns the torrents only).
