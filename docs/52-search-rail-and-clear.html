An empty rail, no way to the plugins, rows that stay

D5s D20 D21 The search view as it is

Search after one search, with one plugin installed. Between the views and the servers the rail holds nothing, so it reads as empty (D5s). The bar's 1 plugin ▾ only picks which of the installed plugins to ask; nothing in the view leads to Settings › qBittorrent › Search plugins, where a second one is installed (D20). There is no × or clear: esc empties the field, then leaves, and coming back shows the last search's rows until the next one (D21).

Why the rows stay. SR12 keeps each server's last results for the session, so a switch of server or view doesn't lose them. That is right for a search you come back to, but nothing lets you end one: esc in the field clears the words only (SR14), and an empty field's esc leaves the view. The words go; the sentence, the plugins' line and the rows stay.
2 · D5s D20 · the rail on search, and the way to plugins

The rail holds the plugins, and the plugins lead to their pane

D5s a · D20 a Plugins, kind and recent in the rail; "install a plugin…" at the plugins' foot

As the log's rail holds its levels (C32 D5), the search rail holds what narrows the answers. plugins: every installed plugin, a tick for whether this search asks it, and how it answered, with the plugins' line's dot and words (a count, … while asking, slow, failed, skipped). A click ticks or unticks it and asks again, as a change in plugins ▾ does today; plugins ▾ and the pills leave the bar and the line, and the line keeps 1 of 2 plugins didn't answer ›. The group's foot row, install a plugin…, opens the pane with its install field focused. kind: any, or one of the types the plugins declare; one pick, which asks again (type ▾ moves here). recent: this server's last searches this session; a click asks it again.

Folded (ctrl+b, or a narrow window). A 48 px rail can't hold a plugin's name and answer, so the bar takes them back: 2 plugins ▾ and any type ▾ return (at 720 px inside filters ▾, as SR6), and the pills return to the line. plugins ▾ gains one last row, manage plugins…, opening the same pane. So there is a way to the plugins in every width.

Recommended: D5s a, D20 a. The plugins are what a search is made of, so they get the room, and the place you see them answer is where you add one. It also moves two filters out of a crowded bar, as the log's levels moved. Recent searches stay in memory only (search words can be private), never saved. It amends C32 D5 (search's rail, left open) and C26 SR6 (plugins ▾ and type ▾ leave the bar while the rail is open) and SR10 (a second way into the pane besides Settings and the empty state).

3 · D21 · clearing

A way to end a search

D21 a "× esc" at the field's end, as the title bar's filter

While the field has words or there are results, it ends in × esc, as the filter field does since C32 D18. A click, or esc in the field, clears the words and the results together, stops asking any plugin still looking (the jobs omaseed started are deleted, SR13), and shows the face before search. One more esc leaves. Recent searches keep the words (D5s a), so nothing is lost that you'd want back.

Recommended: a. It is the model the window already has (the filter's × esc), it sits where the eye is when you want to start over, and esc keeps its one meaning: back out a step. It amends C26 SR14 (esc's order: pane → rows → the words and results → leave search).

D21 b A "clear" word at the end of the results line

The line ends in clear; esc stays as today. Easy to see once you look at the line, but it sits away from the field, and esc still leaves the rows behind.

D21 c Results aren't kept

Leaving search drops the results; coming back shows the face before search, with the last words selected in the field so enter asks again. No new control, but a server switch or a look at the library loses a search that took a minute to fill. It amends C26 SR12 (each server's last results kept for the session).
