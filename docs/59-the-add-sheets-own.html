The add sheet's own

Board 39 drew an arrival sheet: a bar of what's inside, a category guessed for you, a time to ready, and a guard against a "film" that carries a program. Board 46 kept today's sheet instead (C24 H7) and left those four for a board of their own, drawn in its frame. This is that board. It keeps today's sheet and its order, and asks of each of the four: is it something omaseed knows, or something it would be making up? Three of them can be built on facts: what's inside from the file names, the program check from the same, a category from your own library's trackers. The ready time can't, so this board leaves it out. It also draws the magnet's wait, which #172's comment calls the sheet's weakest state.

1 · today, in an omabox

A form around a file tree, and a wait that counts seconds

  • The rows, in order (Views/AddSheet.qml): magnet, URL or file · board 25's files-on-disk line · name · category | tags (label on Deluge, labels on Transmission, C25 K13) · save to, with the free line and the disk's driver line (C17 D8) · layout | limits · options · files. The foot: cancel, add stopped, add.
  • Category. Nothing is preselected. Picking one on qBittorrent fills save to with its folder (unless you typed a path). On Deluge a label's folder is where it moves when done, so save to stays the default (K27). Transmission has no one-per-torrent group, only labels.
  • Files. A .torrent of more than one file gets the tree, the head's count (7 files · 1.84 GB, or 6 of 7 files) and C28's extras line: 2 extras: an NFO and a sample (12 MB). Ticked, so the torrent seeds whole. core/TorrentExtras calls a top-level .exe or .lnk an advert, so a program beside a video is said today, dimly, as an extra, and ticked.
  • A magnet (C5): qBittorrent and Deluge are asked for its list (App.addLookup, Views/AddFetchLine.qml): fetching to 10 s, still looking to 60 s, then no list after N in yellow. On Transmission, one dim line: its files are picked in files after the add (K26).
  • What omaseed knows before the add, for sure: the source's name, size, file names and sizes (a .torrent at once, a magnet once its list is in), its trackers and private flag, and the library: every torrent's name, category or labels, trackers and save path. What it doesn't: the swarm. No engine scrapes a tracker for a torrent it doesn't hold, and qBittorrent's fetchMetadata answers the metainfo only. It doesn't know the library's file lists either: the sync carries none.

3 · A1 and A2 · what's inside

39's type bar, said in words

A1How what's inside readsrecommended a

a: one line of words, the kinds by size, biggest first, the first with its size: 1 video, 1.79 GB · 2 subtitles · 3 extras. At most three kinds, then · 4 other files. b: the same words over a bar in one tone (f18 segments, the skipped ones f8), as wide as each kind's bytes. c: 39's stacked bar, a colour per kind.

Why a: the line answers what is this? at a glance, and the words carry what a bar would only hint at. c needs a colour per kind, and every Theme colour already means a state (green landed, yellow waiting, red wrong): a magenta video would read as something. b's bar is a shape you then have to read the words to understand; in a 600 px sheet the words alone are shorter.

A2Where it sits, and what it countsrecommended a

a: in the Files head, left of today's count, the kinds of the ticked files, so it follows a tick as the count does. Narrow, it elides before the count does. A one-file torrent has no Files row and gets no line: the name in the head already ends in its kind. A magnet's line comes with its list. b: a row of its own, Inside, under the source field: seen first, but a row the sheet grows by on every add. c: in the head's subtitle after the size: it is the first thing read, and it pushes the name out of a subtitle that already elides.

Its screen reader name is the line itself, read after Files: Files, 1 video, 1.79 GB, 2 subtitles, 3 extras, 6 files, 1.84 GB.

Recommended: A1 a, A2 a. What's inside is a line of words where the files already are, built from the file names alone, for every engine and every source that has a list.

4 · A3 to A5 · the program check

A video doesn't need a program to play

A torrent named like a film that carries codec_pack.exe, or a .lnk beside an album, is the oldest trick for getting a program onto someone's machine. Today the sheet says it dimly, as one of the extras, and ticks it. The facts are all in the file list: which files are programs, which are videos or audio, and how big each is. Nothing here guesses at what the name promises.

A3When it speaksrecommended a

a: when the biggest kind is video or audio (A1's first word) and any file, at any depth, is a program. A game, an app or a disc image never sets it off, so the line stays rare and means something. b: any program in any torrent. Every software torrent would warn, and a warning on every add is one nobody reads. c: only a program at the top level, today's advert place. A program one folder down (Extras/setup.exe) would pass.

A4What it says and offersrecommended a

a: one yellow line above the tree, with skip it; nothing is unticked until you press it (C28's rule: the sheet never unticks behind your back), and add stays add. The words:

  • A video with a program inside: codec_pack.exe, 1.4 MB. Dim: A video plays without one. A program in a video's torrent is how most torrent malware arrives. Link: skip it.
  • Music: Music with a program inside: …, dim Music plays without one. … Several: A video with 2 programs inside: setup.exe and codec.msi, 3.0 MB., link skip them.
  • After the skip, the line turns green: Skipped codec_pack.exe. Dim: It won't download, so this torrent won't seed whole. Link: undo.

b: the program is unticked when the list loads, and the line says so with tick it. Safer for someone who never reads the sheet, but then the sheet changes what's added on a rule, and C28 ruled against that for extras. c: the add needs a confirm, add anyway. A second sheet for a file that a skip makes harmless.

Not said: that the video is too small for a film. The sizes are in the line, and the user can read 1 video, 2.1 MB in the Files head for themselves; omaseed doesn't judge what a size should be.

A5A program is no longer an extrarecommended a

a: TorrentExtras' adverts keep .url, .website and .html; .exe and .lnk leave them for A1's programs. In a video or music torrent A4's line says them; anywhere else they are what the torrent is. C28's extras line then never names a program. b: both lines name it, the extras line dimly and A4's in yellow.

Recommended: A3 a, A4 a, A5 a. One yellow line, only where a program has no business, built from the file names, and the user's tick decides.

5 · A6 to A8 · the guessed category

Where your torrents from that tracker usually go

Board 39 guessed from resolution, kind, tracker. Of those, omaseed knows only one for every torrent in the library: its trackers (qBittorrent's tracker map, each Transmission and Deluge torrent's own list). The resolution is a parse of a name, and the library's kinds would need every torrent's file list, one call each.

A6What it is guessed fromrecommended a

a: the trackers. Take the library's torrents (on this server) that announce to any of the source's tracker hosts; when at least 3 of them, and at least 3 in 4, share one category, that's the guess. Otherwise there's none. A busy public tracker that carries everything falls under the 3-in-4 line on its own, so it never guesses. A magnet's tr= hosts are known at once, so its guess doesn't wait for the list. A bare hash has no trackers and no guess. b: the last category you picked in this sheet, kept until you pick another: simple, often right, and often wrong without saying why. c: the trackers and the kind together. It needs the library's file lists, so it can't be built from what omaseed knows.

A7How it readsrecommended a

a: picked, with its reason under the row: the chip is on as if you had picked it (save to follows its folder, as a pick does), and one dim line says why, with a way back: guessed: 7 of your 8 torrents from tracker.films.example are in films · clear. A pick of your own, or clear, takes the line away; clear leaves no category, as today. b: offered, not taken: a chip films? in the row, one click to take. Nothing changes unless you say so, but the add is no faster than today. c: picked with no line. A wrong guess you can't see is worse than none.

A save path you typed is never replaced (today's rule for a pick). The guess is made once, when the source loads; a server switch (C12) makes it again on the new server's library.

6 · A9 · the ready time

Left out: nothing knows the swarm before the add

A9A time to ready in the sheetrecommended a

Board 39 drew ready: about 9 min · 40 seeds · at your usual 3.2 MB/s. Neither number exists before the add: no engine reports seeds for a torrent it doesn't hold, and omaseed's ledger keeps speeds, not how long a download took or how fast this swarm is. a: no ready time in the sheet. Once the torrent is in, the rows display's fact is already ready in 7 min · to ready, worked out from the real swarm (C24 H3, RowWords). b: a dim sum in the free line, at your speed now, 3.2 MB/s: about 9 min. It's arithmetic on a speed other torrents are using, made to look like a promise. c: 39's block, with numbers omaseed would have to invent.

7 · A10 · the magnet's wait

Say what's known, what's coming, what you can do

A10The waitrecommended a

a: a short checklist where the tree will be, in the Move sheet's check lines (C15):

  • ✓ From the link: Night Train · dim 3 trackers · not in your library (the dn, the tr= count, the library check C5 already makes). A link with no dn says no name in the link.
  • ✓ Category guessed: films · dim from tracker.films.example, only when A6 has a guess.
  • ⋯ today's line, Asking qBittorrent for the file list, with its words for each phase unchanged (C5 F1–F8), then one dim line: Then its size, what's inside and the program check.

The Files head's right side keeps the phase and seconds (fetching · 4 s) and turns yellow at a minute, as today. When the list comes, the tree takes the checklist's place, and the head's left gets A1's line. The checklist grows under the rows you are filling, so nothing you are typing moves. b: today's line, with the seconds hidden until 10 s: calmer, but the wait still says nothing of what's known. c: today's.

What you can do meanwhile is already said by today's lines (pick where it goes; add takes every file, add stopped stops it at its list, D4, D5; on qBittorrent 5.3 the add waits for the list, #193), so A10 adds no new advice. On Transmission there is no lookup and no guess (A8): its K26 line stays as it is.

8 · A11 and A12 · a batch, and Transmission's magnets

Where there is no Files row

A11In a batchrecommended a

A batch (docs/27 B1–B3, #40) has no Files row and one category for all. a: no kinds line and no guess; a torrent A3 would speak for gets a yellow a program inside on its row, and one by one, already in the head, opens it with its own sheet and A4's line. It is still added if you don't. b: nothing in a batch; the program goes in unseen. c: a torrent with a program leaves the batch and waits for a sheet of its own after it, as a hand-off does.

A12A magnet on Transmissionrecommended a

Transmission finds a magnet's files only after the add (K26), so the sheet has no list to check. a: nothing more in this board: the sheet's K26 line stays, and a check after the add (in details' files, or a notice when the list lands) is named in §9 for later. b: the K26 line adds the program check can't run before the add here. Honest, but a warning about a check that may have nothing to find.

11 · building the recommended calls

Five pieces

  1. What's inside. core/FileKinds, pure: A1's table, a split set counted once, and the line's words (kindsText(files, wanted), three kinds then N other files). AddFilesModel.kindsText; the Files head draws it, eliding first. tst_filekinds: each row of the table, the split set, the order by size, singular and plural, ticks followed.
  2. The program check. core/ProgramCheck, pure: A3's rule over FileKinds, A4's words for video, music, one and several. TorrentExtras drops .exe and .lnk (A5; its test updated). AddFilesModel gives the programs and a skip that unticks them, with undo; AddSheet draws the line above the tree; the batch list marks a row (A11). tst_programcheck, tst_AddSheet's new cases.
  3. The guess. core/CategoryGuess, pure: tracker hosts in, the library's (hash, hosts, category) rows, the 3 and 3-in-4 rule, out a category and its counts, or none. App feeds it from the tracker map (qBittorrent) or each torrent's trackers (Deluge); none on Transmission (A8). AddSheet picks the chip, draws the line and clear, never touching a typed save path; again after a server switch. tst_categoryguess: public tracker, tie, under 3, two hosts, a bare hash.
  4. The wait. Views/AddFetchLine.qml gains the two check lines and the Then… line (A10), fed from parseAddable's name and trackers, the library check and the guess. tst_AddFetchLine: each phase with and without a name, with and without a guess, Transmission's K26 line with a guess.
  5. Walks and the visual check. A swarm fixture of a video with an invented codec_pack.exe (scripts/dev/swarm.sh), and a playground category that 3 of 4 tracker-sharing torrents have. magnet-walk.sh reads the checklist and the guess; a new leg of actions-walk.sh (or add-walk.sh) reads the kinds line, the program line, skip and undo, and the guess's clear; deluge-walk.sh reads are labelled. The visual check gets an add-loaded screen in both skins.
