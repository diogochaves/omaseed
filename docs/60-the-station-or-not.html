The station, or not

Board 46 drew the Console skin's overview as a station: a numbered plate per category down the left, that category's torrents as small cards beside it, and a column of specials at the right (needs you, next ready, resting). It was never built. A skin paints; it doesn't pick a layout (C31), so a station can't be "what Console shows". It can only be a display, beside rows, table and space, that any skin can show. This board asks whether it should be one. It recommends no, and #199 closes. Everything the station says, the window already says somewhere, and its Omarchy twin, shelves, was dropped in board 51 for saying too little. Sections 3 to 6 draw the station as it would be if you keep it: what it shows, its name, how you pick it, what it does when the window is narrow, and how each skin paints it. If you keep it, those calls are ready too.

Three displays, two skins, one layout each

skin
  • The displays (core/LibraryDisplays): rows, grouped under the five questions (C24 H3); table, one sorted list; space, a size map by folder, then torrent (C32 D12 a). Space took the place of board 46's shelves. A saved overview or shelves reads as space, in state.json and in OMASEED_DISPLAY.
  • How you pick one. The title bar's display tool and its menu: rows, table, space, then the sort block (C32 D13). Or the palette's show as rows, show as table, show as space. There's no key (#168 assumed 8). The pick is remembered per scope: the folder, else the category, tag or tracker, else the question, else the library. A new install starts on rows (C24 H4). OMASEED_DISPLAY pins one for a run.
  • One table under all three. Each display is a face of TorrentTable: the same filtered list, the same cursor, selection and keys. What differs is how the list is grouped (TorrentFilterModel::GroupBy: NoGroups, ByQuestion, BySpace) and how it is drawn. Rows has two narrow steps from its own width (libraryRowsSteps, 620 and 440). Space never scrolls.
  • The skins paint the same three displays. Console is a skin.json of tokens plus its own faces for four seams (Plate, SelectionMark, Meter, Lamp). A component reads a token's value and never asks which skin is drawn; the ctest skin_lint fails on skin === (C31). User skins are the same kind of file, data only (C37).
  • Board 46's station (Console only, overview()): a head line (station ステーション, a find box, the place 01 · films · NAME, a key hint), a plate per category (its number, name and count), the category's cards (two letters, a piece grid, the fact), and a specials column, all on a dotted ground. Its Omarchy twin was shelves: one shelf per category, covers over the piece grid. #171 left the station out (step 6c, point 9) because it is an arrangement, not a form.
  • What board 51 found about that twin (D12): shelves works as the page says, but doesn't earn its place as a display. Rows and the table already answer what is happening, and nothing answered where the space goes. So space replaced it, in both skins.

G1The station displayrecommended a

  • aOut. No station display. #199 closes. Console keeps showing rows, table and space in its own paint, as it does today, and nothing in the app changes. The parts of the station that are form (the cut plates, the voices, the kana) are Console's already, and the parts that are arrangement are dropped.
  • bA fourth display, for every skin, as §3 to §6 draw it: bands by category, a card per torrent, one View that each skin paints with tokens. You pick it like the other three.
  • cThe station in place of space as the third display. That undoes board 51 D12, and the only display that answers where does the space go is lost.

Recommended a. The station tells nothing the window doesn't already tell. Its one new move is to group torrents by category, and that is shelves, which board 51 dropped for both skins. Made a display for every skin, as C31 requires, it brings shelves back to Omarchy under a new name. Kept for Console alone, it would be the skin picking a layout, which C31 forbids. And on Transmission, which has no categories (C14 E4), every torrent would sit in one band. Board 46's station was a picture of Console's mood, and that mood is now in Console's paint on every display.

3 · G2 to G4 · if kept: what it shows

Bands by category, a card per torrent

Sections 3 to 6 apply only if G1 is b. Each call is still made, so a b needs no second board.

G2Its namerecommended a

  • acards. It says what you see, as rows and table do, and it reads the same on every engine. Console's kana for it is カード.
  • bstation, board 46's word. It means something only in Console's mood, and a skin can't rename a display. The words are the app's; a skin adds only kana.
  • ccategories. It names the grouping, but Deluge calls them labels (C25 K13) and Transmission has none (C14 E4), so the tool's word would change with the server.

G3A cardrecommended a

  • aThe name, its fact, a line. The name on up to two lines, then elided. Under it, the rows' fact in the state's colour with its caption (7 min to ready, 1.8× given, didn't move needs you), then the progress line on the Meter seam. 172 spec px wide. A screen reader hears the rows' words: Open Movie Night (2026), 7 min to ready, 87 %.
  • bBoard 46's. Two letters, a 9-column piece grid, the fact. The letters say nothing (OM), and the grid is too small to read.
  • cThe name and the line, no fact: quieter, but you then open the details to learn why a card is red.

G4The specialsrecommended c

  • aBoard 46's column, 160 px at the right. needs you (2 torrents, no access · didn't move), next ready (the name, in 3 min), resting (3 torrents, not sharing). A click picks that question, or puts the cursor on the next-ready torrent.
  • bNone. The rail already counts the questions.
  • cOne line over the bands: next ready distro-2026.09-x86_64.iso in 3 min. A click puts the cursor on it. With nothing arriving the line reads nothing arriving, and below 440 px it keeps only next ready in 3 min.

Recommended: G2 a, G3 a, G4 c. A card should say what a row says, only smaller. Needs you and resting are already in the rail, counted, one key away. The column would take 160 px from every band to say them again. Next ready is the one fact you'd otherwise have to sort for.

4 · G5 and G6 · if kept: picking it, and what goes in a band

A display like the others

G5How you pick itrecommended a

  • aA fourth row in the display tool's menu, after space, with a glyph of its own. The palette row is show as cards. It is remembered per scope as the others are, OMASEED_DISPLAY=cards pins it (station reads as cards), and there's still no key. A new install still starts on rows. The sort block works inside each band, and the tool reads cards · added, as it reads rows · added.
  • bThe third display again, in place of space: G1 c by another door.
  • cThe skin picks it: Console shows cards where Omarchy shows space. Listed only to say why not: C31 rules a skin out of choosing a layout, and a user skin (C37) would then be choosing one too.

G6Bands, and a big categoryrecommended a

  • aEvery card shown, wrapping. One band per category that has torrents in the scope, in the rail's order (Deluge's labels the same way), with no category last, as shelves had it. A band holds all its cards, wrapping onto as many lines as they need, and the display scrolls. j/k walk the cards in band order, as they walk space's blocks. The band's head reads music · 3 torrents · 246 MB. A scope that is one category shows one band.
  • bOne line of cards per band, the rest as + N more, which picks that category in the rail. Tidier, but j and select-all would then skip torrents you can't see.
  • cBands by question: needs you, arriving, and so on. That's the rows display drawn as cards.

Recommended: G5 a, G6 a. If it's a display, it behaves like one: picked in the same tool, remembered the same way, no skin deciding for you. And the keys keep working on everything in the scope.

5 · G7 · if kept: a narrow window

Fold the plate, then the card, never scroll sideways

G7Narrow widthsrecommended a

  • aTwo steps from the list's own width, the rows' steps (620 and 440). Wide: the plate stands at the band's left, the cards flow beside it. Under 620: the plate becomes a head line over its cards (04 · music · 3 torrents · 246 MB in Console, music · 3 torrents · 246 MB in Omarchy), and the cards get the whole width. Under 440: a card takes a whole line, the name and the fact side by side, the progress line under them, much like a row. The specials column (if G4 a) shows only at the wide step. Use the width switch over the mocks.
  • bBoard 46's: each band clips at the right edge, and the specials drop. Cards past the edge can't be seen, and j walks onto them.
  • cRows below 620: the scope draws rows until the window widens. Then a display changes without being picked.

Recommended a. It's the same rule rows already follows, at the same widths. Nothing is hidden, and nothing scrolls sideways.

8 · out of scope

Not on this board, whichever G1 you pick

  • Console's dotted ground (board 46 .ovw's dot grid). A texture under a View is a new kind of token, not a display. A skin board of its own can take it, for every display at once.
  • Grouping rows by category (a group by in the display tool). It's the one new idea hiding in the station, and it's simpler than a fourth display. It needs its own board and isn't drawn here.
  • Arrangements made by a user skin: other layouts of a view from a person's folder. That is faces as code, which C37 U2 keeps out.
  • Covers and artwork: posters or thumbnails for a torrent. omaseed has no source for them.
  • The specials as a panel elsewhere (in the seed console, in the bar widget). Those are different surfaces with their own boards.
  • Space, rows and the table are unchanged. Board 51's ruling on space stands under every option except G1 c.

One piece, or four

With G1 a, one piece:

  1. The record. The DECISIONS entry for this board, with C31's and C37's #199 lines pointed at it, and #199 closed. Docs only; no code, no walk, no baseline moves.

With G1 b, four pieces, each one builder step:

  1. The grouping. TorrentFilterModel::ByCategory (the rail's category order, no category last, the sort inside each band), LibraryDisplays gaining cards (station read as it), OMASEED_DISPLAY=cards. tst_torrentfiltermodel and tst_librarydisplays.
  2. The View. Views/LibraryCards.qml and its card, a face of TorrentTable (cursor, selection, j/k in band order, r), G7's two steps, G4's line. The display tool's row and the palette's show as cards. A tst_LibraryCards.qml.
  3. The paint. G8's tokens in Theme.qml with Omarchy's values, Console's values and カード in its skin.json, skin.schema.json regenerated (C37 U4). skin_lint stays clean; tst_qml_console covers the Console pass.
  4. Walks and baselines. swarm-walk.sh's display pass gains cards. The visual check gains a cards checkpoint in both skins (new baselines to approve), and docs/manual-tests/ gains the lines for a person's pass.
